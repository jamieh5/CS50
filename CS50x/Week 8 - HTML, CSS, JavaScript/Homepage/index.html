History

The beginnings (1950s)

Formula 1 as a World Championship began in 1950. The first race took place on 13 May 1950 at Silverstone in Great Britain. Giuseppe Farina won the first Drivers’ Championship that year with Alfa Romeo. The 1950s belonged to Juan Manuel Fangio from Argentina, who won five titles in total, a record that stood for decades. Cars had front-mounted engines and almost no safety equipment.
